# OULAD — Student Engagement and Retention Analysis

## Business Problem
Understand when students disengage from online courses and identify engagement patterns associated with higher withdrawal rates, with the goal of finding opportunities to improve student retention.

## Analytical Approach
Analyze course withdrawal rates, 7-day activation, frequency of learning material usage, and weekly engagement retention. Compare these metrics across courses and student groups to identify when disengagement occurs and which students may be at greater risk of withdrawal.

## Business Value
Use the findings to recommend potential product improvements, such as timely engagement reminders or additional learning support for struggling students. These recommendations would require further testing to determine whether they improve retention.
